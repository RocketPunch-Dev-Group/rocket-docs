## Searching for a place to stay

1. Enter your destination, check-in and check-out dates, and guest details, then search.
2. Use the filter and sort options to find a place that matches what you want.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Filter | Star rating, hotel review score, property type, price per night, distance from the city center, amenities, and more |
| Sort | Recommended, review score, biggest discount, distance to major attractions, price low to high, price high to low |
