Activity collects every event you registered a ticket for. Each event shows its name, the dates it runs, and your current attendance status.

## Attendance status

Tickets that require host approval are not confirmed as soon as you register them. The host's decision appears as your attendance status, so you can check here whether you were approved. Tickets that do not require approval are confirmed the moment you register.
