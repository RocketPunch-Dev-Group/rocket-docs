If you come across an inappropriate post or comment, you can report it. Please report anything that does not comply with the [Community Guidelines](https://www.rocketpunch.com/terms?termType=COMMUNITY) or that you believe has legal or ethical issues.

1. Select the `More button ⋮` on the post or comment and select `Report Post` / `Report Comment`.
2. Select a reason for the report and press `Submit`.
   | Reason | Description |
   | --- | --- |
   | Insulting language toward another person | Profanity, slander, or ridicule used to put down a specific user. |
   | Prejudice or hate speech about race, gender, religion, and so on | Content that discriminates against a specific group or person, or encourages hatred. |
   | Offensive or sexually explicit language or images | Content that includes language or images that are sexually degrading. |
   | Encouragement or depiction of self-harm or suicide | Content that encourages self-harm or suicide, or describes it in detail. |
   | Sharing criminal plans or glorifying terrorism | Content that plots a crime or justifies terrorism. |
   | Exposing personal information such as identity or location without consent | Someone's real name, contact details, or location was made public without their consent. |
   | Posting someone else's work without consent | Someone else's work was taken and posted without permission. |
   | Free text (up to 500 characters) | Enter your own reason in 500 characters or less if none of the reasons above apply. |
3. The post or comment you reported is hidden from your screen right away, and the report is handled after an internal review by our operations team.

<Note>
  Your report stays anonymous, and the content is no longer recommended to you after you submit it. If the situation is serious, please also report it to your local authorities.
</Note>
