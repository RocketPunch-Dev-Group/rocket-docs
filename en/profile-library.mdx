## Fill up your library

Your library is where you keep **files and URLs that introduce you better**, such as resumes, portfolios, and cover letters. You can find your library on your profile. Files and links saved in your library can be selected and attached right away when you Quick Apply to a job posting.

### Add a file

Choose a type (Resume, Portfolio, Cover letter, or Custom Input) and attach your file. hwp, hwpx, doc, docx, ppt, pptx, xls, xlsx, pdf, and key formats are supported, and you can upload up to 100MB.

### Add a URL

Choose a type and enter the URL. Along with resumes, portfolios, and cover letters, you can pick social types such as LinkedIn, Github, Instagram, Threads, X, and Facebook, or enter your own.

## Set library visibility

You can set the visibility of each PDF or link in your library individually.

| Visibility | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Public | Every Rocketpunch member can see it. |
| Recruiters only | Visible to recruiters or headhunters who can send you offers. |
| Private | Only used when you apply to a job posting, and other users cannot see it. |
