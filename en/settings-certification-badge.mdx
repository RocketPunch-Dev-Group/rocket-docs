The verification badge shows the credibility of creators and businesses active on Rocketpunch. It is currently in beta.

## Who can get a badge

- **Active Engagement:** Provided to profiles that have contributed to activating the community.
- **Influencer:** Provided to profiles that have committed to consistently posting content on Rocketpunch.
- **Enterprise Membership Company:** Provided to company profiles that have subscribed to a membership to promote their business.

## Apply

You can apply from `Settings` \> `Activity` \> `Verification Badge`. If you believe your profile should have a verification badge, tap `Apply` to fill out the [creator / business verification badge application form](https://partner.rocketpunch.com/verified-badge).
