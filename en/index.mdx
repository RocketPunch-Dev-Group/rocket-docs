Rocketpunch is a **networking platform** built around a new way of connecting people. It goes beyond a simple hiring service, bringing people, companies, content, events, and opportunities together in one place.

Rocketpunch started in 2012 as South Korea's first startup hiring platform, and has grown into a platform that follows you through many moments of your career and business — profiles, jobs, events, content, and Wallet.

To see the path Rocketpunch has taken, read [Rocketpunch history](/en/start-history).

## What can you do on Rocketpunch?

## Ready to start?

1. [**Create an account**](https://www.rocketpunch.com/login) — Create a Rocketpunch account and log in.
2. **Fill in your profile** — Complete a profile that introduces you, with your career and interests.
3. **Follow** — Follow the people, companies, and topics you care about to build your own feed.
