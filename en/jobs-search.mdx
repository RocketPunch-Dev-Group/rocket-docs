Rocketpunch Jobs has a lot of postings. Keyword search, filters, and sorting help you find the right one quickly. This page shows you how to search postings and apply filters and sorting.

## Searching by keyword

Enter a keyword in the search bar at the top of the Jobs screen. Results are matched against the job title, the subtitle, the name of the company that posted the job, and the body of the posting.

## Applying filters

You can narrow your search with filters. You can select several options within a single filter, and tapping `Apply` updates the results. The more filters a posting matches, the higher it appears.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Job Category | Software development, data/AI, planning/strategy, design/UX, and other fields you are interested in |
| Seniority | \[Entry level\], \[Junior\], \[Mid-level\], \[Senior\], \[C-level\] |
| Company Size | Very small (under 30 employees)<br />Small (30-100 employees)<br />Medium (100-400 employees)<br />Large (400-2,000 employees)<br />Very large (over 2,000 employees) |
| Work Type | Always on-site (no remote work)<br />Hybrid (1-4 remote days a week)<br />Fully remote (occasional on-site work included) |

## Sorting

You can sort results by `Relevance`, `Popularity`, `Latest`, or `By Company Size`. Relevance shows the postings that best match the criteria you entered first, and By Company Size shows postings from larger companies first.

Ads that companies have paid for may appear at the top of the list with an `Ad` tag. For details, see the [Rocketpunch recommendation transparency terms](https://www.rocketpunch.com/terms?termType=TRANSPARENCY_OF_RECOMMENDATION).

<Tip>
  If you want to post jobs or ads yourself, you can get started on the [enterprise service](https://enterprise.rocketpunch.com).
</Tip>

## Found an inappropriate job posting?

Select the `More ⋮` button on a posting to report it or block the person who posted it. For the reporting steps and how reports are handled, see [Reporting job postings and courses](/en/report-jobs-edu).
