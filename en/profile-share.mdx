## Your profile URL

Your profile URL is built from your handle (ID). For example, if your handle is `steve`, your profile URL is `www.rocketpunch.com/@steve`. Copy this URL and paste it into a message, an email, social media, and anywhere else you want to share it.

If your handle changes, the old URL automatically points to the new one, so links you have already shared keep working.

## Short profile URL

Add `.rockets.app` to your handle to get a short profile URL. For example, if your handle is `steve`, the short URL is `steve.rockets.app`. Use it on your resume, other social media, business cards, and more.

<Tip>
  Your short profile URL is also used as your wallet address. For details, see [Wallet overview](/en/wallet-overview).
</Tip>

## Share with a QR code

For people nearby, you can show your profile as a QR code. Select the QR icon on your profile screen to display the code. When someone scans it, they can open your profile and follow you.
