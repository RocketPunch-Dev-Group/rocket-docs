Rocketpunch supports World ID human verification to build a space for real people rather than bots or fake accounts. This page explains what World ID is and how to verify with it on Rocketpunch.

## What is World ID?

World ID is a proof-of-personhood method provided by World. It lets you prove that you are a verified, unique user — not a bot or a fake account — without revealing other information such as your name or contact details. It is separate from [Verify your account](/en/account-identity-verification), which confirms that one person uses one account; World ID proves that the person behind the account is a real human.

Once you complete human verification on Rocketpunch, you can access more profiles, posts, and conversations.

## Add human verification to your Rocketpunch profile

You can add human verification from `Settings > My Info & Login > Human Verification` by selecting `Verify`. To add human verification, you need a World ID that has already completed human verification at an Orb. If you don't have a World ID yet, see below for how to get one.
