In `Settings` \> `Billing & Invoices`, you can register the payment methods used for Rocketpunch paid services and check your past payments and invoices.

## Add and remove payment methods

Use `Add Payment Method` to register a card. You can save up to five payment methods. Your primary payment method appears at the top of the list, and you can tap the `More button ︙` on each payment method to make it the primary one or remove it.

### Cards you can register

You can register credit cards, check cards, and debit cards. Cards issued by American Express (AMEX) can't be registered.

| Type | Card companies |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic (Korea) | Shinhan Card · Samsung Card · Hyundai Card · KB Kookmin Card · Lotte Card · Woori Card · Hana Card · NH Nonghyup Card · BC Card |
| International | VISA · Mastercard · UnionPay · JCB |

<Note>
  Where you register a corporate card depends on the name printed on it. Register a **named corporate card**, which shows the employee's name alongside the company name, as a `Personal card`; register an **unnamed corporate card**, which shows only the company name, as a `Corporate card`.
</Note>

## Check your billing history

Your payments are listed newest first, and you can check each payment's status (Upcoming Payment · Completed · Failed · Canceled). You can download the invoice for each entry as a PDF file.
