Once you find a program you like, you can apply through its application page. Besides applying, you can also share the posting with someone else.

## Applying

Applications for courses are handled on each institution's own application page. Select `Go to application page` on the course page to go to the page the institution provides, and follow the institution's instructions for how to apply and what is required.

The course page shows information about the institution along with the Capacity, the status, and the Self-payment amount. Courses do not offer Quick Apply, so follow the institution's instructions for how to apply and what the requirements are.

### Checking your course application history

You can find it under `Settings` \> `External Applications` \> `Education Courses`.

Selecting `Go to application page` counts as applying, so the course is recorded here. If you did not actually apply or you do not need the record, remove entries one at a time with `Delete`, or all at once with `Clear All History`. Deleting a record does not affect an application you actually submitted on the external site.

## Sharing

Select `Share` to copy the course link and share it with someone else.
