You can view Rocketpunch in the language, display currency, and timezone that work for you. Set them in `Settings` \> `Language & Region`.

## Set your language

When you pick a display language, the Rocketpunch interface appears in that language.

<Info>
  **Supported languages:** Korean, English, Spanish, Chinese (Traditional and Simplified), Japanese, Vietnamese, Thai, German, French, Portuguese
</Info>

### Automatic content translation

When you change your language, content written in another language is **translated automatically** into the language you picked. Automatic translation is on by default, and you can turn it off if you prefer. For translated content, tap `View Original` on each item to see it in its original language.

With automatic translation on, the body of a piece of content is translated into the language you picked. It works in Threads, Jobs, Events, and more — but Discover only supports translation into English.

## Set your currency

Pick the currency that amounts are shown in on Rocketpunch. The value of the assets you hold in Wallet is also converted and shown in this currency.

<Info>
  **Supported currencies:** 173 currencies, including Korean won (KRW), US dollar (USD), and euro (EUR)
</Info>

Your display currency only changes how amounts appear on screen — it does not change the assets you actually hold or your payment methods. You can also change your display currency from Wallet.

## Set your timezone

Time information such as event schedules and post timestamps is shown based on the timezone you pick.

<Info>
  **Supported timezones:** Timezones for 29 major cities including Seoul, Tokyo, London, Paris, and Sydney (based on UTC offset)
</Info>
