Discover is the fastest way to see news from around the world, gathered from more than 1,000 sources. News refreshes every minute, so you can follow breaking stories on everything from AI and industry trends to market conditions, and read news organized by the topics you care about, along with summaries.

## What is Discover?

Discover groups several reports on the same event into one item and shows you a summary of the key points.

- Each item pulls together articles from several sources, shown on the card as "N sources".
- News is divided by topic (category).
- Discover shows only the latest news. To see past news again, you need to save it in advance.
- Each item shows an impact score. AI assigns it based on how prominently several outlets covered the story and its social and economic reach; it has nothing to do with view counts or reactions.
