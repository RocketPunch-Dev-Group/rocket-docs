`Activity` \> `Events you joined` collects every event you registered a ticket for. Each event shows its name, the dates it runs, and your current attendance status.

To learn how to register a ticket, see [Register an event ticket](/en/event-ticket-purchase). To cancel a registered ticket or get a refund for a paid ticket, see [Cancel an event ticket](/en/event-ticket-refund).

## Attendance status

Tickets that require host approval are not confirmed as soon as you register them. Until the host reviews your registration, the status shows `Pending approval`; once the host is done, it changes to `Approved`. Tickets that do not require approval are `Approved` the moment you register.

## Check-in QR code

Once your attendance is confirmed, a QR code is issued. On the day of the event, open the event here to find the QR code, and use it to check in at the venue.
