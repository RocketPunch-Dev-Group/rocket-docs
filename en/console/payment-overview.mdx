`Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Billing` is where you manage payment methods and your billing history. Managing payment methods requires the owner role or higher.

## Registering a payment method

Select `Add credit/check card` to add a card.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Card type | Corporate card or personal card |
| Card number | The number shown on the card |
| Expiration date | In MM/YY format |
| First 2 digits of the password | The first two digits of the card password |
| Business registration number (10 digits) | Enter this when registering a corporate card. |
| Date of birth (YYMMDD) | Enter this when registering a personal card. |

All recurring payments are charged to your primary payment method. The card set as the primary payment method cannot be removed directly; make another card the primary payment method first, and then you can remove it.

### Cards you can register

You can register credit cards, check cards, and debit cards. Cards issued by American Express (AMEX) cannot be registered.

| Type | Card companies |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic (Korea) | Shinhan Card · Samsung Card · Hyundai Card · KB Kookmin Card · Lotte Card · Woori Card · Hana Card · NH Nonghyup Card · BC Card |
| International | VISA · Mastercard · UnionPay · JCB |

<Note>
  Where you register a corporate card depends on the name printed on it. Register a **named corporate card**, which shows the employee's name alongside the company name, as a `Personal card`; register an **unnamed corporate card**, which shows only the company name, as a `Corporate card`.
</Note>

## Reviewing upcoming payments

You can see what is scheduled to be charged next, along with the date and the amount.

## Reviewing your billing history

Review the list of past payments, or select the `More button ···` on an entry to download its invoice. If you have questions about a payment, contact the Support Center.
