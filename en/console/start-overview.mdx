## What can you do in the Enterprise console?

The Enterprise console is where a company registered on Rocketpunch manages hiring, branding, and advertising in one place. Select `Enterprise Services` on Rocketpunch to open it.

The console opens on the dashboard. It shows your current plan and subscription period, the number of active job postings, the list of admins, and recent messages on a single screen.

## Ready to start?

1. [**Create a company profile**](/en/console/profile-overview) — If your company is not on Rocketpunch yet, create its profile.
2. [**Invite members**](/en/console/permission-invite) — Invite the people you work with and split up permissions by area.
3. [**Post a job**](/en/console/jobs-create) — Post a Quick Apply job for free and start receiving applications.
