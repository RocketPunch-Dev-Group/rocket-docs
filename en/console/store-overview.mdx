The Rocketpunch store is where catering, venue rental, promotional items, and brand stores are sold. Companies hosting events order and pay for the products in a store.

Stores are opened only for vendors who have arranged it with Rocketpunch in advance. Until then, the `Store` menu does not appear in the sidebar. If you want to use it, contact us at [support@rocketpunch.com](mailto:support@rocketpunch.com).
