Open your event in `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` and manage registrations in `Guests and queue` on the event.

## Registration status

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | A registrant waiting for host approval. |
| Invited | A guest the host invited. |
| Approved | A guest whose attendance is confirmed. |
| Rejected | A registrant whose request was turned down. |
| Not checked in | A guest whose attendance is confirmed but who has not checked in yet. |
| Checked in | A guest who arrived and was let in. |
| No-show | A guest whose attendance was confirmed but who did not come. |
| Canceled | A guest who canceled their attendance. |

If you did not turn on `Host approval required` when creating the ticket, registrations are approved the moment they are made.

## Managing registrations

The list shows the guest, ticket type, registration time, and registration status. Review the registrants who are pending and select the `✓ button` to approve them or the `X button` to reject them.

You can search by name or email, filter by status, and sort by registration date. You can also export the guest list as a CSV file.

Attendance overview shows a count for each guest status. Select the `Check-in` button to start checking guests in. For details, see [Managing event check-in](/en/console/event-check-in).

Tickets with a limited quantity also show how many remain. Select `Ticket management` to manage them.
