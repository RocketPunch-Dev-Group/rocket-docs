  The console uses "admin" in two ways:
  1) the value of an area permission (None / Admin)
  2) the "Additional admin" and "Invite admin" labels on events
  In this documentation, "member" refers to a person and "admin" refers to a permission value. */}

Add teammates from `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Permissions` by selecting `Invite`. Inviting members requires the owner role or higher.

## Inviting a member

1. In `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Permissions`, select `Invite`.
2. Enter the Rocketpunch handle of the person you want to invite.
3. Set their base permission. You can choose owner or member.
4. Select their area permissions. You can pick more than one of social, hiring, events, education, and advertising.
5. Select `Invite` to finish.

<Tip>
  Even when you want to hand over the **primary owner role**, you have to finish the invitation first. After that, select the member in the list under `Permissions` and **transfer** the primary owner role to them.
</Tip>

For what each permission covers, see [Understanding permissions](/en/console/permission-roles).

## Granting other permissions

You can put someone in charge of a single posting or event without giving them permissions for the whole company. Once granted, they appear in `Permissions` \> `Item-level access`.

| Item | Permission |
| :-- | :-- |
| [Posting reviewer](/en/console/jobs-applicants) | Reviews the posting and its applicants, and changes an applicant's status. |
| [Additional event admin](/en/console/event-host) | Manages everything about the event, from its overview to its tickets, or reviews the guest list and runs check-in. |
