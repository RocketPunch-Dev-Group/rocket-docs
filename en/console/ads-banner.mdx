You can run an ad in `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Advertising` \> `Start advertising` by selecting `Get started`.

If you want a consultation or a quote, contact us at [business@rocketpunch.com](mailto:business@rocketpunch.com) or fill in the [advertising inquiry form](https://ads.rocketpunch.com/apply). To run an ad yourself, follow the steps below.

## Banner ads

### Choosing a banner type

Pick where the ad appears first. Selecting a type shows a preview.

| Type | Where it appears |
| --- | --- |
| Standard banner | Posts, profiles, company pages, and elsewhere |
| Home banner | At the top of the home screen when someone opens Rocketpunch |

### Preparing your creative

| Field | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Main text | Up to 2 lines, 40 characters |
| Sub text | Up to 40 characters |
| Destination URL | The address the banner opens when selected |
| Background media | PNG, JPG, GIF, MP4, or MOV, up to 5MB <br />If the background image is bright and the text is hard to read, turn on `Darken background`. |
| Logo | SVG or PNG with no background, up to 5MB. Optional. |

### Run dates and how cost is calculated

An ad can run for up to 60 days at a time. The cost is the daily rate multiplied by the number of days. The total appears at the bottom of the setup screen; check it, then select `Start banner ad`.

For details on pricing, check the [Enterprise console](https://enterprise.rocketpunch.com/login).
