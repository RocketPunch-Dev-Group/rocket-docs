<Accordion title="Do we create a separate company account?">
  Creating a company profile requires **a personal Rocketpunch account for the person who will manage it**. Without a Rocketpunch account, you cannot create a company profile.

  One Rocketpunch account can manage several companies, and several people can manage one company together.

  **Do not create a personal account under the company's name.** The personal profile you create in order to hold admin permissions belongs to that admin, while the company profile is the account for the company itself.

  For details, see [Creating a company profile](/en/console/profile-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get permission to manage a company profile?">
  If a colleague already has permission, being invited is the fastest route. See [Inviting members](/en/console/permission-invite).

  If there is nobody to invite you, submit documents proving that you belong to the company and apply yourself, using the [company profile permission request](https://support.rocketpunch.com/company-permission) form. For details, see [Requesting permission for a company with no members](/en/console/permission-request).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The previous admin left the company and we cannot get into the account.">
  You do not need to take over their account. The new admin can apply for permission with their own account.

  Do not create a new company profile. Splitting into two profiles confuses the people who visit them. For details, see [Requesting permission for a company with no members](/en/console/permission-request).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My permission request was rejected.">
  Check the fields on the request form again. A company profile URL and an admin's account URL use different formats.

  | Field | Format |
  | --- | --- |
  | Company profile URL on Rocketpunch | `www.rocketpunch.com/companies/companyhandle` |
  | Account URL of the person receiving permission | `www.rocketpunch.com/@handle` |

  Supporting documents must have been issued within the last 3 months.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We want to change who manages the profile.">
  Invite the new admin as a member, then hand over the primary owner role in the `Change permissions` window.

  For details, see [Managing member permissions](/en/console/permission-manage).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we hire through Rocketpunch?">
  Once you create a company profile, you can post jobs for free. You can also post events and courses, and run ads.

  To get started, see [Getting started](/en/console/start-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where is Enterprise Services?">
  Select `Enterprise Services` on Rocketpunch to open the Enterprise console.

  You need permission to manage a company profile to reach the console. Without it, the menu does not appear.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I edit our company information?">
  Edit it in `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Profile` \> `Company information`.

  The fields are locked by default. Select `Edit` in each section before you can type in them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="There is nowhere to edit our headcount or founding date.">
  The fields only open after you select `Edit` in the `Company details` section. Until then, every field looks locked.

  For details, see [Editing company information](/en/console/profile-basic).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="There is someone on our company profile we do not know.">
  The People list shows users who added your company to the career history on their own profile. Because each entry is career history the user created, your company cannot delete it.

  You can hide them from your company profile instead, or change their employment status and dates. For details, see [People at your company](/en/console/profile-employees).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is free and what is paid?">
  | Item | Free / Paid |
  | --- | --- |
  | Quick Apply job postings | Free. No limit on how many. |
  | Placement fee | Free. 0% on every plan. |
  | Outlink job postings | Growth plan and above |
  | Boosted job posting visibility | Growth plan and above |
  | Free event listings | Free. No limit on how many. |
  | Paid event ticket sales fee | 3% on Free, 0% from Growth up |
  | Banner ads | Paid. Billed per day. |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a fee when we make a hire?">
  The placement fee is 0% on every plan. Even on the free plan, no placement fee is charged.

  For details, see [Plans](/en/console/plan-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many job postings can we publish?">
  Every plan lets you run an unlimited number of postings at once. Quick Apply postings are free. Outlink postings, which send applicants to your own careers site, are available from the Growth plan up.

  For details, see [Plans](/en/console/plan-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we only use the Free plan for a year?">
  The Free plan has no time limit. The active period shown on the dashboard is the current plan's coverage window.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We paid but our plan has not changed.">
  If the payment went through and the Plans screen still shows the previous plan, contact the Support Center.

  Telling us your company name, the plan you paid for, and the payment date speeds things up.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do we post a job?">
  Post it in `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Hiring` \> `Create job posting`.

  For details, see [Posting a job](/en/console/jobs-create).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A posting we created is not in the list.">
  The posting list is split into tabs by status. Drafts are under `Drafts`, and postings whose start date has not arrived are under `Scheduled`. Postings past their posting period move to `Closed`.

  If it is not under `Drafts` either, saving the draft did not finish. After you select `Save draft`, stay on the page until the job posting list appears.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do we review applicant information?">
  Open the posting and check `Applicants`. Select an applicant to open or download the documents they submitted.

  For details, see [Managing applicants](/en/console/jobs-applicants).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We want to delete our company profile.">
  Company profile deletion is submitted through a request form. You cannot delete it from the console yourself.

  For what happens after deletion, see [Deleting a company profile](/en/console/profile-delete).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We cannot delete our account.">
  You cannot delete a personal profile while you are registered as a company member.

  In `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Permissions`, select `Remove permission` to remove your company permissions first, and then delete your account.

  For details, see [Deleting a company profile](/en/console/profile-delete).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We deleted it but it still shows up in search engines.">
  It takes time for search engines to catch up. If you want it hidden right away, you can submit a request to each search engine.

  - Google: [Remove web search results from Google Search](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/11080680?hl=en)
  - Naver: [Request removal of a site (post) from search results](https://help.naver.com/inquiry/input.help?categoryNo=5575&serviceNo=5626&lang=en)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What changes if we pay annually?">
  It is about 17% cheaper than monthly billing, and each plan comes with extra advertising credit. If you withdraw within a year, unused credit is returned.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we change plans at any time?">
  An upgrade applies immediately, and a downgrade applies after your current billing cycle ends.

  For details, see [Plans](/en/console/plan-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we sign up for the Enterprise plan?">
  It runs through a consultation tailored to your company. Select `Contact sales` on the Plans screen to request one.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Changing the language">
  In `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services`, select `🌐︎` in the top bar to open the language list. Pick 한국어, English, or 日本語 and the screen switches immediately.
</Accordion>
