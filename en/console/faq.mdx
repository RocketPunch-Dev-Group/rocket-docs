<Accordion title="Do we create a separate company account?">
  Creating a company profile requires **a personal Rocketpunch account for the person who will manage it**. Without a Rocketpunch account, you cannot create a company profile.

  One Rocketpunch account can manage several companies, and several people can manage one company together.

  **Do not create a personal account under the company's name.** The personal profile you create in order to hold admin permissions belongs to that admin, while the company profile is the account for the company itself.

  For details, see [Creating a company profile](/en/console/profile-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get permission to manage a company profile?">
  If a colleague already has permission, being invited is the fastest route. See [Inviting members](/en/console/permission-invite).

  If there is nobody to invite you, submit documents proving that you belong to the company and apply yourself, using the [company profile permission request](https://support.rocketpunch.com/company-permission) form. For details, see [Requesting permission for a company with no members](/en/console/permission-request).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The previous admin left the company and we cannot get into the account.">
  You do not need to take over their account. The new admin can apply for permission with their own account.

  Do not create a new company profile. Splitting into two profiles confuses the people who visit them. For details, see [Requesting permission for a company with no members](/en/console/permission-request).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My permission request was rejected.">
  Check the fields on the request form again. A company profile URL and an admin's account URL use different formats.

  | Field | Format |
  | --- | --- |
  | Company profile URL on Rocketpunch | `www.rocketpunch.com/companies/companyhandle` |
  | Account URL of the person receiving permission | `www.rocketpunch.com/@handle` |

  Supporting documents must have been issued within the last 3 months.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We want to change who manages the profile.">
  Invite the new admin as a member, then hand over the primary owner role in the `Change permissions` window.

  For details, see [Managing member permissions](/en/console/permission-manage).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we hire through Rocketpunch?">
  Once you create a company profile, you can post jobs for free. You can also post events and courses, and run ads.

  To get started, see [Getting started](/en/console/start-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where is Enterprise Services?">
  Select `Enterprise Services` on Rocketpunch to open the Enterprise console.

  You need permission to manage a company profile to reach the console. Without it, the menu does not appear.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I edit our company information?">
  Edit it in `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Profile` \> `Company information`.

  The fields are locked by default. Select `Edit` in each section before you can type in them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Changing the language">
  In `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services`, select `🌐︎` in the top bar to open the language list. Pick 한국어, English, or 日本語 and the screen switches immediately.
</Accordion>
