<Accordion title="Do we create a separate company account?">
  Creating a company profile requires **a personal Rocketpunch account for the person who will manage it**. Without a Rocketpunch account, you cannot create a company profile.

  One Rocketpunch account can manage several companies, and several people can manage one company together.

  **Do not create a personal account under the company's name.** The personal profile you create in order to hold admin permissions belongs to that admin, while the company profile is the account for the company itself.

  For details, see [Creating a company profile](/en/console/profile-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get permission to manage a company profile?">
  If a colleague already has permission, being invited is the fastest route. See [Inviting members](/en/console/permission-invite).

  If there is nobody to invite you, submit documents proving that you belong to the company and apply yourself, using the [company profile permission request](https://support.rocketpunch.com/company-permission) form. For details, see [Requesting permission to manage a company profile](/en/console/permission-request).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The previous admin left and we cannot get into the company profile.">
  You do not need to take over their account. The new admin can apply for permission with their own account. For details, see [Requesting permission to manage a company profile](/en/console/permission-request).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I am leaving the company. What do I need to hand over?">
  Invite your successor as a member first, then remove your own permission. Only the primary owner and owners can invite members.

  If you are the primary owner, hand the primary owner role to another member before you can remove your permission. For details, see [Managing member permissions](/en/console/permission-manage).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My permission request was rejected.">
  Filling in the request form incorrectly can get your request rejected. Check that you used the right format for each field. Supporting documents must also have been issued within the last 3 months.

  | Field | Format |
  | --- | --- |
  | Company profile URL on Rocketpunch | `www.rocketpunch.com/companies/companyhandle` |
  | Account URL of the person receiving permission | `www.rocketpunch.com/@handle` |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We want to change who manages the profile (the primary owner).">
  Invite the new admin as a member, then change the primary owner in the `Change permissions` window. For details, see [Managing member permissions](/en/console/permission-manage).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where is Enterprise Services?">
  Select `Enterprise Services` on Rocketpunch to open the Enterprise console.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I edit our company information?">
  Edit it in `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Profile` \> `Company information`. Select `Edit` in each section before you can type in it. For details, see [Editing company information](/en/console/profile-basic).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="There is nowhere to edit our headcount or founding date.">
  The fields only open after you select `Edit` in the `Company details` section. Until then, every field looks locked. For details, see [Editing company information](/en/console/profile-basic).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="There is someone on our company profile we do not know.">
  The People list shows users who added your company to the career history on their own profile. Because each entry is career history the user created, your company cannot delete it.

  You can hide them from your company profile instead, or change their employment status and dates. For details, see [Managing people at your company](/en/console/profile-employees).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is free and what is paid?">
  Each plan comes with different benefits. Posting a Quick Apply job and creating an event are free. For details, see [Plans](/en/console/plan-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a fee when we make a hire?">
  Rocketpunch does not charge a placement fee. For details, see [Plans](/en/console/plan-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many job postings can we publish?">
  Every plan lets you run an unlimited number of postings at once. Posting a Quick Apply job is free. For details, see [Plans](/en/console/plan-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We paid but our plan has not changed.">
  If the payment went through and the Plans screen still shows the previous plan, contact the Support Center.

  Telling us your company name, the plan you paid for, and the payment date speeds things up.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do we post a job?">
  Post it in `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Hiring` \> `Create job posting`. For details, see [Posting a job](/en/console/jobs-create).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A posting we created is not in the list.">
  The posting list is split into tabs by status. Drafts are under `Drafts`, and postings whose start date has not arrived are under `Scheduled`. Postings past their posting period move to `Closed`.

  If it is not under `Drafts` either, saving the draft did not finish. Leaving the page after you select `Save draft` but before the job posting list appears can cause the draft to fail.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do we review applicant information?">
  Open the posting and check `Applicants`. Select an applicant to open or download the documents they submitted.

  For details, see [Managing applicants](/en/console/jobs-applicants).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We want to delete our company profile.">
  Company profile deletion is only possible in specific cases, and is submitted through a request form. For details, see [Deleting a company profile](/en/console/profile-delete).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We cannot delete our account.">
  You cannot delete a personal profile while you are registered as a company member.

  In `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Permissions`, select `Remove permission` to remove your company permissions first, and then delete your account.

  For details, see [Deleting a company profile](/en/console/profile-delete).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We deleted it but it still shows up in search engines.">
  It takes time for search engines to catch up. If you want it hidden right away, you can submit a request to each search engine.

  - Google: [Remove web search results from Google Search](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/11080680?hl=en)
  - Naver: [Request removal of a site (post) from search results](https://help.naver.com/inquiry/input.help?categoryNo=5575&serviceNo=5626&lang=en)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we change plans at any time?">
  An upgrade applies immediately, and a downgrade applies after your current billing cycle ends. For details, see [Plans](/en/console/plan-overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the Enterprise console support other languages?">
  In `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services`, select `🌐︎` in the top bar to open the language list. 한국어, English, and 日本語 are supported.
</Accordion>
