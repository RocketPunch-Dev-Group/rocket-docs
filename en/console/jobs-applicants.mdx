Open a posting in `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Hiring` \> `Job postings`, then check `Applicants`.

## Application status

An applicant moves through six stages: Application received, Passed document screening, Passed interview, Hired, Application not selected, and Hiring stopped.

A new application starts as `Application received`; you move it through the later stages yourself. `Days waiting` counts the time since the applicant first applied, which helps you find applications that have been sitting untouched.

<Warning>
  If you do not change an applicant's status for **28 days or more**, the status **changes to "Hiring stopped"** automatically. Update the status on those applications yourself.
</Warning>

## Reviewing an application

Select an applicant to open their application. It shows their name, Rocketpunch profile, phone number, email, job category, and seniority, and you can open or download the documents they submitted right on the screen.

`Progress` shows when they applied and their current status, and `Change status` moves them to another stage. You can also write notes on the application; applicants cannot see them.

### Messaging an applicant

Below the application, you can message the applicant in real time and attach files. It is convenient when you want to write while looking at their details and submitted documents.
