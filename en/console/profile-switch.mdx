One Rocketpunch account can manage the profiles of several companies.

## Switching companies

In `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services`, select the company name at the top of the sidebar. The list of companies you have permission to manage opens, and selecting one switches to it.

## Adding a company

Select the company name at the top of the sidebar to open the list, then select `Create company` at the bottom to create a new company profile. For details, see [Creating a company profile](/en/console/profile-overview).
