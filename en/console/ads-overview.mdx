You can review the product list in `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Advertising` \> `Start advertising`.

## Types of ads

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Banner ads" icon="window-maximize">
    Shown as a banner on posts, profiles, company pages, and elsewhere.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Post ads (coming soon)" icon="pen-to-square">
    Ads that appear in the feed in the same form as a post.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Targeted message ads (coming soon)" icon="message">
    Ads sent as in-app messages, emails, and text messages to an audience you target by age, gender, job category, years of experience, education, and similar criteria.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Requesting a consultation

If it is hard to tell which product fits, you can request a consultation.

Select `Get started` under talk to an expert before deciding, in the product list. Enter the product types you are considering, the dates you want to run the ad, your budget range, and your question. Asking costs nothing.

<Tip>
  Every billing cycle of a plan subscription comes with advertising credit equal to the amount you paid. For details, see [Plans](/en/console/plan-overview).
</Tip>
