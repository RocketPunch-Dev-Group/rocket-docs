`Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Store` \> `Manage orders` is where you review incoming orders and quote requests. Choose a service type tab, then pick either `Orders` or `Quote requests`.

Orders arrive per event. The list shows the event name, the items ordered, the amount paid, the requested delivery date, and the order status; selecting an order opens its details on the right. Select `View event` to go to that event.

A customer can add products and pay right away, or skip the products and request a quote by giving only their expected headcount and budget. Quote requests work the same way as orders: you check the contact details and send a payment request. For the customer's side, see [Event catering](/en/console/event-catering).

When an order or quote request comes in, a notification is sent to the contact email registered on your store home.

## Contacting the customer

In the order details, select `View contact details` to see their phone number and email. Use it when you need to arrange delivery or pass along more information.

<Warning>
  For everyone's safety, do not steer customers toward outside payment or cash payment.
</Warning>

## Changing the order status

In the order details, set the order status to `Pending`, `Order approved`, `Delivered`, or `Order canceled`.

**The order status is how your store records its own progress.** Setting it to `Order canceled` does not cancel the payment. To actually cancel it, use `Refund and cancel`.

## Requesting an additional payment

When the amount goes up while you are working with the customer, select `Request additional payment`, enter a title for the request, the amount, how long the request stays valid, and any payment instructions, then select `Send payment request`. Write a clear title and instructions so the customer can tell what they are paying for.

The customer receives a payment link at the phone number and email they entered when ordering. They can pay by credit card, check card, Toss Pay, Kakao Pay, Naver Pay, or PAYCO, and a confirmation email is sent once the payment goes through.

Payment requests you have sent appear in that order's details, where you can send more or refund them one at a time.

## Refunding and canceling

Select `Refund and cancel` at the bottom of the order details. It applies to the amount the customer paid up front; payment requests you sent separately are refunded one at a time under `Payment requests sent`.

| Type | What happens | Reasons you can choose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cancel and refund in full | Cancels the whole order. | Buyer requested cancellation, cannot fulfill due to a product issue, cannot fulfill due to a delivery issue, repayment of the amount, other |
| Partial refund | Refunds only the amount you enter. You cannot enter more than the amount paid. | Refund of the difference after a change to the order, additional discount applied, product price reduced, other |

Choosing `Other` lets you write the reason yourself.

<Warning>
  Refunds and cancellations cannot be undone. The button on a processed order changes to `Refund requested` and cannot be selected again.
</Warning>
