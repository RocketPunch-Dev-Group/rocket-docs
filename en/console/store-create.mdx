## Creating a store

In `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Store` \> `Store home`, select `Create` to create your store.

### Registering the basics

| Field | How to fill it in |
| --- | --- |
| Store logo | Upload an image. A 1:1 ratio and 800px or larger is recommended. |
| Store name | Up to 50 characters. |
| Business registration number | Digits only, no hyphens. Not shown to customers. |
| Business address | Search for it and select it. Not shown to customers. |
| Address details | Enter the building, floor, unit number, and so on. |
| Website | Enter your store address. |
| Store introduction | Up to 500 characters. |

### Posting store news

You can post updates, announcements, and portfolios of past events for customers to see. Select `Add` in the store news section, write a title and body, and attach images. Titles can be up to 100 characters and bodies up to 1,000.

Customers see this news alongside your contact details and website when they select `Store information` on the ordering screen.

### Registering a contact

Enter a contact name, contact email, and contact phone number. Customer inquiries arrive at the contact email, and order notifications, quote request notifications, and settlement notices are sent there too.

### Registering settlement details

Register the account that receives your sales revenue. Choose your bank, then enter the account number and the account holder's name.
