---
title: "Changing the language"
description: "How to change the display language of the Enterprise console."
keywords: ["language", "change language", "language settings", "display language", "Korean", "English", "Japanese", "한국어", "日本語", "localization", "translation", "locale"]
---

The Enterprise console is available in 한국어, English, and 日本語.

In `Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services`, select `🌐︎` in the top bar to open the language list. Pick a language and the screen switches immediately.
