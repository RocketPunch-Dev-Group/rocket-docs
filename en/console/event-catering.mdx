## Requesting a quote

If it is hard to decide on specific items, request a quote first.

1. Select `Request a quote` on the vendor to open the request form.
2. Enter your expected headcount, budget, phone number, and email.
3. Add anything else you need in the notes.
4. Select `Request a quote` to submit.

The event name and event dates are filled in automatically and cannot be changed on the form.
