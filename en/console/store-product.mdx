`Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Store` \> `Manage products` is where you add and manage the products you sell.

## Adding a product

Select `Add product` and enter the product's details.

| Field | How to fill it in |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Products in the same category appear together in the list. You can add your own. |
| Product image | The main image shown next to the product name. A square ratio is recommended. |
| Detail image | The image shown when someone selects the product. It has to be at least 360px wide; a tall image is cropped automatically on upload and appears continuous on the product page. |
| Product name | Up to 100 characters. |
| Product description | Up to 500 characters. |
| Price | Choose between `Enter a price` and `Price on request`. A `Price on request` product can be added to a cart but is not charged: it comes in at ₩0, and you send a separate payment request once you settle on an amount. |
| Discounted price | Optional. If you enter one, the list price and the discount rate are shown alongside it. |

## Stopping sales

Turn off the `Available` switch in the product list and customers can no longer see the product. Use it when something is sold out or you are pausing sales. A deleted product cannot be restored.

## Adding general information

This is the information shown on every product page. You can turn `Product information`, `Ordering information`, and `Delivery information` on and off separately, and select `Edit` to write each one.

It helps to write down what customers must know about a product, how to request a quote, and when and how you deliver.
