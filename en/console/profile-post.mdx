You can publish posts as your company profile. Posting as a company requires the Social permission.

## Switching to your company profile

Open the compose window in the Rocketpunch feed. The profile you are about to post as appears at the top left. Select it to open the profile list, which shows your personal profile alongside every company profile you can manage. Choose the profile you want to post as, write your post, and select `Post`.

Published posts show your company profile as the author. You cannot select more than one profile at a time, and character limits, attachments, editing, and deleting work the same as on a personal profile, so see [Publishing a thread](/en/feed-post) for details.
