You can publish posts as your company profile. Posting as a company requires the Social permission.

## Switching to your company profile

<Frame caption="Figma Multi Profile">
  ![Empty Image](/images/empty_image.png "Empty Image")
</Frame>

Open the compose window in the Rocketpunch feed. The profile you are about to post as appears at the top left, for example `Post as Hyukmin Kim`. Select it to open the profile list, which shows your personal profile alongside every company profile you can manage. Choose the company, write your post, and select `Post`.

Published posts appear in the feed with the company's name and image. Character limits, attachments, editing, and deleting work the same as on a personal profile, so see [Publishing a thread](/en/feed-post) for details.

A post is published from one profile only. You cannot select your personal profile and a company profile at the same time.
