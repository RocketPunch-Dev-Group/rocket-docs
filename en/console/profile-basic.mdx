`Rocketpunch` \> `Enterprise Services` \> `Profile` \> `Company information` is where you manage what appears on your company profile. To make a change, select `Edit` in the section you want to change.

<Warning>
  Your changes are lost if you do not select `Save` after filling in the fields. To discard what you entered, select `Cancel`.
</Warning>

## Company details

Register your representative, financial information, and headquarters location. All of these are optional.

The fields only open after you select `Edit` in the `Company details` section. Until then, every field looks locked. If you cannot find anywhere to type, check whether you selected `Edit`.

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Representative | Enter the representative's name. |
| Capital | Enter the amount in KRW. |
| Annual revenue | Enter the amount in KRW. |
| Headcount | Enter the number of people. |
| Total funding raised | Enter the amount in KRW. |
| Founding date | Pick it from the calendar. |
| Headquarters address | Search for it and select it. |
| Address details | Enter the building, floor, unit number, and so on. |
| Location notes | Enter anything that helps people find you, such as which floor or which entrance to use. |

Select `Save` when you are done.

## Links

You can put your website, social media, app download pages, and similar links on your company profile. Each entry takes **a title and a URL**, and you can register up to 12.
