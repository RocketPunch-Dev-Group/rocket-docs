The first time you log in to Rocketpunch, you set your handle and career. You can change this information later on your profile.

## Set your handle

### Where is the handle used?

- **Profile address:** Your handle becomes your profile URL. (Example: [www.rocketpunch.com/@yourhandle](http://www.rocketpunch.com/@yourhandle))
- **Search and mentions:** You can search for profiles by name and handle, or mention someone by putting their handle after @.
- **Short profile URL:** Add ".rockets.app" to your handle to get a short profile URL. Use it on your resume, other social media, business cards, and so on. <br />(Example: if your handle is `steve` → `steve.rockets.app`)

### Handle rules

- It can be 3 to 30 characters long.
- Only lowercase English letters (a–z), numbers (0–9), and hyphens (-) can be used.
- Hyphens (-) cannot be used consecutively, and cannot come at the beginning or the end.
- A handle that another user is already using is not available.

<Warning>
  Handles that are violent or offensive, spam handles, handles containing sensitive personal information such as a phone number, <br />and handles that impersonate a company or brand on a personal profile are not allowed, and Rocketpunch may change them at its discretion. <br /><br />A company profile is separate from a personal profile. If you want to create one, register it in [Creating a company profile](/en/console/profile-overview).
</Warning>

### Changing your handle

You can change your handle yourself in `My Profile` \> `Edit profile`. Once changed, you have to wait 60 days before you can change it again. Within 30 days of the change, though, you can revert to your previous handle regardless of that limit.

During that period, the old profile address automatically points to the new one. After 30 days, another user can take the old handle.

<Warning>
  **The short profile URL (`.rockets.app`) does not point to the new one.** <br />When you change your handle, the old short URL stops working right away. <br />A short URL for the new handle is created instead, and it can take a moment to start working. <br /><br />If you put a short URL on your resume or business card, update it after you change your handle. <br />If you revert to your previous handle, the old short URL works again.
</Warning>

If you are using a handle created under the old rules, you are prompted to set a handle that meets the current rules the next time you log in. Your handle may also change when you migrate or merge a previous account's profile into your current account.

## Set up your career

After setting your handle, you enter your career. You can change your career settings later.

Choose your **Employment Status**.

<Callout>
  Currently affiliated (Company, research lab, etc.)<br />Not affiliated (Unemployed, part-time, etc.)<br />I am a middle/high school/college (graduate) student
</Callout>

Choose your **Job Category (Desired Job Category)** from the options below.

<Callout>
  Software Development · Data/AI · Planning/Strategy · Design/UX · Marketing/PR · Management/Operations · HR/People · Accounting/Finance · Legal · Customer Service/Sales · Education/Social Welfare · Hardware Development · Research/R&D · Banking/Investment · Healthcare/Bio · Construction/Real Estate · Media/Publishing · Manufacturing/Production · Driving/Logistics · Food/Culinary · Hospitality/Leisure · Service · Religious Work
</Callout>

Choose the **Seniority** that matches your experience.

<Callout>
  Entry level: Intern, or no hands-on experience<br />Junior: Handles basic tasks (skill level of roughly 1–3 years)<br />Mid-level: Solid understanding, owns projects (skill level of roughly 3–7 years)<br />Senior: Deep understanding, leads a team of several people (skill level of roughly 7–15 years)<br />C-level: Expert level, executive decision-making authority
</Callout>

Once you finish setting your handle and career, your initial account setup is complete.
