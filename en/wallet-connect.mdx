Without a private key or secret recovery phrase, you can bring external wallets you already use — such as MetaMask or OKX Wallet — into Rocketpunch and see them together on one screen. You can also do the opposite and register Rocketpunch Wallet with other services.

## Connect an external wallet to Rocketpunch Wallet

In `Manage My Wallets` \> `Import Wallet` \> `Connect External Wallet`, connect through WalletConnect, or connect MetaMask or OKX Wallet directly. Connected wallets appear under `Connected External Wallet`.

This method does not involve entering a private key or secret recovery phrase. It only links the two wallets, so you check your assets on Rocketpunch and approve their use in the connected wallet. If you disconnect, that wallet no longer appears on Rocketpunch.

## Connect Rocketpunch Wallet to an external wallet (or dApp)

You can use Rocketpunch Wallet on external services such as Uniswap and OpenSea. When you mint an NFT or log in to a blockchain service, you sign with Rocketpunch Wallet.

Connect through WalletConnect in `Manage Wallet` \> `dApp Connection`. Scan the QR code shown on the dApp with your camera, or paste the WalletConnect link you copied from the dApp.

This method does not hand over your private key or secret recovery phrase. Each time a connected dApp makes a request, you approve it in Rocketpunch Wallet.
