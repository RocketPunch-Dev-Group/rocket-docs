When you log in for the first time with an email address or a social account, a Rocketpunch account is created for you automatically. If you already have an account, log in the same way. By logging in, you agree to the Rocketpunch Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Whichever method you use, it all connects to the same single account — so start with whatever is easiest for you.

## Starting with a social account

You can get started more easily with a social account you already use.

1. On the [Log in](https://www.rocketpunch.com/login?redirectTo=/) screen, select one of the social login buttons. Google, Apple, Naver, and Kakao are available.
2. Log in by following the instructions from the service you chose. Your Rocketpunch account is created and you're taken to the Rocketpunch home.

## Starting with email

1. On the [Log in](https://www.rocketpunch.com/login?redirectTo=/) screen, enter the email address you want to use.
2. A verification code is sent to that email address. Check the email for the code and enter it, and you move on to the identity verification page.

<Note>
  A verification code is valid for 15 minutes after you receive it. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder, or resend the verification code after 30 seconds.
</Note>
