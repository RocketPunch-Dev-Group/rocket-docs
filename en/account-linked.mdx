## Social login methods you can link

You can log in to Rocketpunch by linking an email address, a social account, and more, and you can check and manage them yourself in Settings. Google, Apple, Naver, and Kakao accounts can be linked for login.

Linking several accounts makes logging in easier, and it also lets you log in with another account [if you can't access your previous login method](/en/account-login-error).

## Manage linked login methods

1. Go to `Settings` \> `My Info & Login`.
2. Under `Linked Accounts`, you can see which accounts are currently linked.
3. Select the three dots `⋮` to the right of an account to unlink that account, or tap `+ Link Account` to link a new login method.
