If you come across an inappropriate job posting or education course, you can report it. Please report anything that does not comply with the [Community Guidelines](https://www.rocketpunch.com/terms?termType=COMMUNITY_GUIDELINE) or that you believe has legal or ethical issues.

## Reporting a job posting

1. On the job posting details, press the `More button ···` and select `Report`.
2. Select a reason for the report and press `Submit`.
   | Reason | Description |
   | --- | --- |
   | A role or company that does not actually exist | The posting is for a position nobody is hiring for, or the company name, location, or working conditions differ from reality. |
   | Inappropriate requirements such as gender or age | Eligibility is limited by conditions that have nothing to do with doing the job. |
   | Excessive requests for personal information (resident registration number and the like) | The posting asks for information that document screening does not require, such as your resident registration number (Korea's national ID number) or family details. |
   | Requests for money or payments when you apply | Applicants are told to pay a fee, such as training costs or a deposit. |
   | A commercial posting unrelated to hiring | The posting exists to promote a product or service rather than to hire. |
   | Free text (up to 500 characters) | Enter your own reason in 500 characters or less if none of the reasons above apply. |
3. The job posting you reported is hidden from your screen right away, and the report is handled after an internal review by our operations team.

## Reporting a course

1. On the course details, press the `More button ···` and select `Report`.
2. Select a reason for the report and press `Submit`.
   | Reason | Description |
   | --- | --- |
   | Course information that differs from reality | The course is not actually recruiting, or the schedule, cost, or host organization differs from what is stated. |
   | Inappropriate requirements such as gender or age | Eligibility is limited by conditions that have nothing to do with the course content. |
   | Excessive requests for personal information (resident registration number and the like) | The course asks for information that registration does not require, such as your resident registration number (Korea's national ID number) or family details. |
   | Requests for money beyond the stated tuition | You are asked to transfer or pay extra money on top of the announced cost. |
   | Pressure to join a religion or group, or take part in activities unrelated to the course | Instead of teaching, the course pushes you to join a particular religion or organization, or asks you to bring people you know. |
   | A commercial posting unrelated to education | The posting exists to promote a product or service rather than to enroll students. |
   | Free text (up to 500 characters) | Enter your own reason in 500 characters or less if none of the reasons above apply. |
   |  |  |
3. The course you reported is hidden from your screen right away, and the report is handled after an internal review by our operations team.

<Note>
  Your report stays anonymous, and the content is no longer recommended to you after you submit it. If the situation is serious, please also report it to your local authorities.
</Note>
