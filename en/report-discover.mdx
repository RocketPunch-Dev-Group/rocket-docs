If you come across an inappropriate Discover item, you can report it. Please report Discover items that do not comply with the [Community Guidelines](https://www.rocketpunch.com/terms?termType=COMMUNITY_GUIDELINE) or that you believe have legal or ethical issues.

1. Select the `More button ⋮` on the Discover item and select `Report Discover`.
2. Select a reason for the report and press `Submit`.
   | Reason | Description |
   | --- | --- |
   | Contains false or distorted information | The news presents unverified claims as fact, or reports the facts inaccurately. |
   | A title or summary that differs from the actual content | What the title and summary announce differs from the original article. |
   | Contains offensive or explicit language or images | The news contains violent language or images, or ones that are sexually degrading. |
   | Promotional news posted as advertising | It looks like news, but it actually promotes a particular product or service. |
   | News that infringes copyright or other rights | As the rights holder of the work, you believe it was used without credit, or beyond the scope you allowed. |
   | Free text (up to 500 characters) | Enter your own reason in 500 characters or less if none of the reasons above apply. |
3. The report is handled after an internal review by our operations team.

<Note>
  Your report stays anonymous, and the content is no longer recommended to you after you submit it. If the situation is serious, please also report it to your local authorities.
</Note>
