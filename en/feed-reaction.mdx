## Reacting to a thread

You can react to a thread with Boost, comments, reposts, and shares.

### Boost

Boost is how you show that you like a thread. Threads with more Boosts reach more people. Select Boost again to undo it.

### Comments

You can join the conversation by leaving an opinion or a question on a thread.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Character limit | Up to 2,000 characters. |
| Images | You can attach one image per comment. |
| Blocked users | Comments and replies written by users you have blocked are not shown. |

You can reply to a comment to keep the conversation going. When you write a reply, the author of the original comment is mentioned automatically.

You get a notification when someone replies to your comment. Only the author can delete their own comment or reply, and deleting an original comment also deletes the replies to it.

### Repost

You can add your own take to a thread you liked and share it on your feed. You can repost the same thread only once, and clicking the repost button again undoes the repost.

For details, see [Posting a thread](/en/feed-post).

### Share

You can share a thread by copying its link.

## Found an inappropriate post?

Select the `More button ⋮` on a post to report it or block its author. For the reporting steps and how reports are handled, see [Reporting a post](/en/report-post-reply).
