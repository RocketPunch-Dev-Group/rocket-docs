You can log in to Rocketpunch in several ways, but each person may use only one account. Identity verification is how we confirm this.

## What is account verification?

Account verification is the process of confirming that one person is using one Rocketpunch account. Once you finish verifying, a verification label appears on your profile, showing that you are a real user rather than an automatically generated account.

Even when you use different login methods, they connect to a single account as soon as verification confirms you are the same person. So no matter how many login methods you link, you still have just one account.

<Tip>
  **The verification method differs by nationality**, so verify your account using the method that fits your situation. See the details below.
</Tip>

## South Korean nationals

### Verify with a phone number

Verify with a phone number issued by a South Korean carrier (SKT, KT, LG U\+, and MVNOs). You need to be able to receive text messages (SMS) sent from Korea.

<Note>
  Phone number verification runs securely through NICE ID, and ID verification through Persona, Sumsub, and Stripe. <br />For details, see the [\[Account Verification and Protection Policy\]](https://www.rocketpunch.com/terms?termType=IDENTITY_VERIFICATION_POLICY).
</Note>

### If you changed your phone number or your legal name

If you changed your phone number or carrier, or legally changed your name, South Korean nationals are logged in automatically without a new account being created. If you need to update your personal information, go to `Settings` \> `My Info & Login` and change it through account verification.

<Info>
  If you have trouble verifying, or if you need to change the name on the account, contact the Support Center.
</Info>

## Other nationalities (including South Korean nationals staying outside South Korea)

If you are not a South Korean national, or you are a South Korean national staying outside South Korea, you can verify your account with an ID.

### Verify with an ID

Verify with a passport, driver's license, or government-issued ID.
