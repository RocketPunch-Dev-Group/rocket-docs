In Discover you can browse news on the topics you care about, and save or share the stories you like.

## Finding news you care about

- Select a topic in the category tabs at the top of the screen to see only the news for that topic.
- Select `For Me` to customize your Discover topics around your interests.
- Discover Summary gives you the gist quickly, and Sources shows where the original article came from.
- Discover only shows the latest news, so save a story in advance if you want to read it again later.

## Reacting

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Save for later | Saves the story so you can come back to it. |
| Share | Copies a link to the story so you can share it. |
| Repost | Posts the story to your feed to share it. |
| More | Choose `Not interested in this topic` to get fewer recommendations like it. |

## Found something inappropriate?

If there is a problem with a story in Discover, you can report it. See [Reporting Discover content](/en/report-discover) for how.
