---
title: "If you can't access your previous login method"
description: "What to do when you forgot your password or can no longer access a login method you linked before."
keywords: ["forgot password", "lost password", "password reset", "reset password", "can't log in", "login not working", "forgot login method", "lost login method", "account recovery"]
---

As long as you complete identity verification, you can get back into the account you used before.

For example, if you signed up with a Google account but can no longer use it, log in with an Apple account and complete identity verification — you will be connected to your existing account.

<Tip>
  Once you have logged in by linking another social account, go to `Settings` \> `My Info & Login` \> `Linked Accounts` to register new login methods or unlink existing ones.
</Tip>
