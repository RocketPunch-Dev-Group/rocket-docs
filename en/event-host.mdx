Hosting your own event requires a company profile.

For how to create one, see [Creating a company profile](/en/console/profile-overview). Once your profile exists, create your event in [Creating an event](/en/console/event-create).

<Tip>
  **Can we create a company profile without being a registered business?**

  Yes — even an individual hosting an event has to create a company profile. The business registration number and the company details are optional, so leave them blank, and pick whichever industry is closest to the field you work in.

  A company profile is separate from your personal profile, so keep your personal account under your own name and register the name you go by on the company profile.
</Tip>
