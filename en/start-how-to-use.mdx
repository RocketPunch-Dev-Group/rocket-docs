## Finding what you need quickly

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Search for it" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Type your question into the search box at the top of the screen. Press Ctrl K (or ⌘ K) to open it right away.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Ask the AI" icon="sparkles">
    Select `Ask Assistant` and ask your question as a sentence. It puts together an answer based on this guide.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Browse the menu" icon="bars">
    Use the left sidebar to find instructions for each vertical service.<br />
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
