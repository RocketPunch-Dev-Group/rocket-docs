## Privacy

In `Settings` \> `Security and Privacy` \> `Cookie Settings`, you can turn the cookies Rocketpunch uses on or off by category.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Necessary Cookies | Required for the service to work. Can't be turned off. |
| Functional Cookies | Remember the choices you made, such as your username, language, and region, and apply them to what you see. They're also used to recommend other content based on the pages you visited and the content you read. |
| Marketing Cookies | Used to show more relevant ads, limit how many times the same ad appears, and measure how well ads perform. These are third-party cookies provided by advertising partners. |
| Analytics Cookies | Used to measure how the service is used and how it performs, and to identify technical issues. |

## Active sessions

In `Settings` \> `Security and Privacy` \> `Active Sessions`, you can see the devices currently logged in to your account, along with the device type, location, and time of access. You can log out a specific device, or log out of all devices except the one you're using now.
