## Reporting an inappropriate profile

If you come across an inappropriate profile, you can report it. Please report profiles that do not comply with the [Community Guidelines](https://www.rocketpunch.com/terms?termType=COMMUNITY_GUIDELINE) or that you believe have legal or ethical issues.

1. On the profile of the user you want to report, press the `More button ···` and click `Report`.
2. Select a reason for the report and press `Submit`.
   | Reason | Description |
   | --- | --- |
   | False information about education, work experience, or credentials | A company they never worked at is listed as work experience, or a degree or certificate they never earned is posted. |
   | An account that uses someone else's name, photo, or career history | An impersonation account that pretends to be another person. |
   | An account that appears to belong to a user under 15 | The user appears to be below the minimum signup age set in the Terms of Service. |
   | Discriminatory language in the bio or elsewhere | The profile contains expressions that demean a group or person because of race, gender, religion, and so on. |
   | Free text (up to 500 characters) | Enter your own reason in 500 characters or less if none of the reasons above apply. |
3. The profile you reported is hidden from your screen right away, and the report is handled after an internal review by our operations team.

## Blocking a profile

You can block a specific user or company. On their profile, or on a post or comment they wrote, press the More button `···` and press `Block Profile`.

Blocking changes the following:

- Their content is no longer recommended in your Feed.
- You no longer receive notifications about their activity.
- If you were following them, you are unfollowed.
- Neither of you can send messages to the other.

## Unblocking a profile

In `Settings` \> `Blocked Profiles`, you can see the users you blocked and unblock them.

## Reporting a chat

If you receive an inappropriate message, you can report it. Please report messages that do not comply with the [Community Guidelines](https://www.rocketpunch.com/terms?termType=COMMUNITY_GUIDELINE) or that you believe have legal or ethical issues.

1. Right-click the message you want to report, or press and hold it on mobile, and select `Report User`.
2. Select a reason for the report and press `Submit`.
   | Reason | Description |
   | --- | --- |
   | Insulting language toward another person | You received a message that puts you down with profanity, slander, or ridicule. |
   | Requests for money transfers, phishing links, and the like | Someone asks you to send money on the pretext of an investment or a loan, or sends a link meant to steal your personal information. |
   | Bulk or irrelevant advertising messages | You repeatedly receive promotional messages that have nothing to do with the conversation. |
   | Offensive or sexually explicit language or images | You received language or images that are sexually degrading. |
   | Free text (up to 500 characters) | Enter your own reason in 500 characters or less if none of the reasons above apply. |
3. The message you reported is hidden from your screen right away, and the report is handled after an internal review by our operations team.

<Note>
  Your report stays anonymous, and the content is no longer recommended to you after you submit it. If the situation is serious, please also report it to your local authorities.
</Note>

## Reporting an impersonated profile

If you find a profile impersonating you or someone you represent, file a report through [**Report an impersonated profile**](https://support.rocketpunch.com/identity-theft). You need the address of the impersonating profile and documents that verify your identity. We review what you submit and send the result to the email address you entered.
