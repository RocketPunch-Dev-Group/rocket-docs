## View blocked profiles

You can see the users and companies you blocked, and unblock them. Blocking does the following:

- Their content is no longer recommended in your Feed.
- You no longer receive notifications about their activity.
- If you were following them, you are unfollowed.
- Neither of you can send messages to the other.

To learn how to block someone, see [Report or block a profile](/en/report-profile).
