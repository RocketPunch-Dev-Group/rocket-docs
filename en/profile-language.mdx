## Registering a language

You can register the languages you speak. Registering your languages helps you receive more offers.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Language | Search for a language and select it, or enter one yourself. |
| Proficiency | Beginner, Intermediate (can communicate at work), Upper-intermediate (communicates smoothly at work), Advanced (communicates freely), Native |
